# 机器人自主作业课题组 - 马云开副研究员个人主页

## 中文介绍

**关于本网站**  
本网站是中国科学院自动化研究所、多模态人工智能系统全国重点实验室、机器人系统研究团队下属 **机器人自主作业课题组** 的官方学术主页，同时也是 **马云开副研究员** 的个人主页。

网站旨在全面展示课题组的科研方向、团队成员、科研成果、教学与招生信息等，为中英文访客提供清晰、便捷的学术资源访问入口。页面采用响应式设计，支持中英文双语切换，适配桌面端与移动端浏览。

**主要功能模块**  
- **个人概况**：包含马云开老师的个人简介、联系方式、教育背景、工作经历及学术链接（Google Scholar、ORCID、IEEE 等）。  
- **科学研究**：介绍课题组主要研究方向（智能机器人、微装配、机器人自主作业、具身智能、三维视觉等）及开设课程。  
- **科研项目**：列出承担的国家级、省部级重要科研项目（国家科技重大专项、国自然青年基金、博士后基金等）。  
- **论文专著**：展示代表性期刊与会议论文（IEEE TII、TIE、TASE、ICCV、ICRA 等），并提供下载链接。  
- **科技成果**：汇总已授权的发明专利与软件著作权。  
- **荣誉及奖励**：包括学术兼职（CAAI、CCCC 委员）、科技奖励、审稿服务等。  
- **招生信息**：发布实习生招聘、研究生招生要求与联系方式。

## English Introduction

**About This Website**  
This website is the official academic homepage of the **Autonomous Robotic Operation Research Group**, which belongs to the **Robot System Research Team** at the **State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems**, **Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**. It also serves as the personal homepage of **Associate Professor Yunkai Ma**.

The site is designed to comprehensively present the research directions, team members, scientific achievements, teaching, and recruitment information of the group. It provides a clear and convenient access point for both Chinese and English visitors. The page is fully responsive and supports bilingual switching for an optimal viewing experience on desktops and mobile devices.

**Key Sections**  
- **Profile**: Includes Yunkai Ma’s brief biography, contact information, education, career history, and academic links (Google Scholar, ORCID, IEEE).  
- **Research**: Covers main research interests (intelligent robotics, micro-assembly, autonomous operation, embodied intelligence, 3D vision) and taught courses.  
- **Projects**: Lists major national and provincial research projects (National Major S&T Projects, NSFC Youth Fund, Postdoctoral Foundation, etc.).  
- **Publications**: Showcases representative journal and conference papers (IEEE TII, TIE, TASE, ICCV, ICRA, etc.) with download links.  
- **Achievements**: Summarizes granted invention patents and software copyrights.  
- **Honors & Awards**: Includes academic services (CAAI/CCCC committee members), awards, and reviewing activities.  
- **Recruitment**: Provides information for internship and graduate student positions, including contact details.

[https://yunkaima.github.io/](https://yunkaima.github.io/)
